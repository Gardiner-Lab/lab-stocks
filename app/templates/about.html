Lab Stocks {{ version }}

Trellis Tools' glycerol stocks, primers, chemicals and antibodies.

  • Glycerol stocks, numbered per lab (GL-G-0042) and linked to the plasmid they hold. Confirming a plasmid by sequencing in the Plasmid Editor makes its glycerol stock here.
  • Primers (GL-O-0007) with their sequence, length, GC and Tm, and where the tube is. Primers designed in the Plasmid Editor can be saved here, and the editor shows which bind a plasmid.
  • Chemicals (GL-C-0003) with their CAS number, room, size and safety data sheet, and an Out switch for one that has run out.
  • Antibodies (GL-A-0005): short code, primary or secondary, producer, source animal, product number and notes about the use.
  • Orders: anyone asks for what the lab needs; the lab's lab admin orders it. Chemicals switched to Out are added by themselves.
  • Storage, labels with QR codes, photos and files, and links from the Lab Notebook.

A lab's stocks are shared within the lab and not seen by other labs. A lab's orders are seen by its lab admins, and each request by the one who made it.
