Lab Stocks {{ version }}

The Gardiner Lab suite's glycerol stocks, primers and chemicals.

  • Glycerol stocks, numbered per lab (GL-G-0042) and linked to the plasmid they hold. Confirming a plasmid by sequencing in the Plasmid Editor makes its glycerol stock here.
  • Primers (GL-O-0007) with their sequence, length, GC and Tm, and where the tube is. Primers designed in the Plasmid Editor can be saved here, and the editor shows which bind a plasmid.
  • Storage, labels with QR codes, photos and files, and links from the Lab Notebook.

A lab's stocks are shared within the lab and not seen by other labs.
