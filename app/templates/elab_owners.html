Which lab each eLab owner belongs to

A shared eLab group holds several labs' samples. When a sample is brought over, it becomes the stock of the lab whoever made it in eLab belongs to — not automatically the lab doing the bringing over. Someone not listed here stays with the lab bringing the samples over, and is credited to the administrator.

Changing someone's lab here does not move the stocks already brought over; it decides where the next ones go.
